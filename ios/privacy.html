Midnight Signal - iOS

Privacy policy and support. Effective October 17, 2026.

Your data stays on your device

The iPhone and iPad app contains all 24 puzzles and works offline. Honghong Games does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data from this app. It includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, account login or third-party SDK.

Puzzle progress and language preferences are stored locally in WebKit storage. The haptics setting is stored in local app preferences. Deleting the app removes local data. There is no account or cloud sync, so we cannot recover deleted progress.

Purchases and support

The App Store handles purchases and refunds under Apple policies. We do not receive your payment card details. If you contact us on our support page, the information you post is handled by that website under its own privacy policy. Do not post private information publicly.
