iPhone and iPad privacy policy and support. Effective October 18, 2026.

Your data stays on your device

This is a complete offline game. Honghong Games does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data from the app. It has no ads, tracking, analytics, third-party SDK, login or live AI service. Progress, language and game records stay in device WebKit storage. Haptic preferences stay in local app preferences.

Export uses Apple’s system share sheet so you can choose where to save a JSON backup. Import reads only the file you select and validates it before replacing progress. No game data is sent to us. Deleting the app removes local saves; there is no cloud sync or account recovery.

Purchases and support

Apple handles purchases, payments and refunds under its own policies. We do not receive payment card details. For a problem, open an issue using the support link below, include the game title, device and steps to reproduce. Do not post personal or payment information. GitHub handles information you voluntarily post under its privacy policy. You can also use the App Store’s Report a Problem flow for purchase assistance.
